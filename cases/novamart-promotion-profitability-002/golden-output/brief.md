# NovaMart promotion profitability review

Holiday Sale produced more total completed-order merchandise gross profit: $67,286.45 compared with $30,445.47 for Black Friday. Black Friday produced more merchandise gross profit per promotion day: $4,349.35 compared with $3,958.03. The rankings differ because Black Friday ran for 7 days while Holiday Sale ran for 17 days.

Before deciding which promotion to repeat, marketing should investigate whether either promotion created incremental demand or shifted purchases that would otherwise have occurred, along with customer mix and costs not included in merchandise cost. This descriptive comparison does not prove causation or incremental profit.
